How DAgger turns failures into training data

Each round rolls the current policy out on 800 rehearsals, snapshots every failure, keeps the most uncertain within a balanced budget, and lets the scripted expert record a recovery from each kept state. Round 3 below harvests with the promoted round-2 policy.

Round 1 put recoveries at 3.5% of training and stayed flat. Round 2 upweighted them ×8 to about a third. Failures fell from 138 to 112, almost all in no_contact (57 → 33).

FLUX predicts the right actions and still fails closed-loop

Open-loop mean action error on 60 held-out demo frames, by horizon. The LoRA beats holding the pose three to one at 32 steps; zero-shot FLUX is no better than standing still. In closed loop the same LoRA scores 50 of 576, with 358 of 526 failures never touching the block.

What is not established

  • Nothing has run on the physical arm. The repo will be updated with live results when it arrives.
  • New-object generalisation has not moved: held_out_objects stays at 47–52 of 96 for every candidate.
  • Round 2's gain is significant on one suite alone; the pooled gain passed a gate fixed in advance.
  • FLUX's failure mechanism (8-frame self-history and integrated deltas) is a hypothesis, not tested by retraining.
  • so101-benchmark stays at 0–1 of 50 for every policy.
  • One benchmark seed and one training seed per checkpoint.
