Scoreboard

DAgger rounds 2 and 3 each cleared the gate: 464, then 483 on the retrain (the first round-3 run scored 481). Rounds 4 and 5, with RL-expert recoveries at ×8 and ×2, dropped back to 471 and 455. Real co-training (v9) loses 65; FLUX, trained on the same demos for the same volume, collapses.

Where round 2 gained

Better on five of six suites, significant on one; held-out objects did not move.

Grey dot is v5, coloured dot is round 2. The labels count scenes where only one checkpoint succeeded.

How it fails

Two DAgger rounds cut no_contact from 57 to 24. Holding rose from 4 to 18 in round 2 and eased to 13 in round 3: the policy grasps but does not always let go on target.

Each bar is one checkpoint's failures, split by the label FailureWatch assigns. FLUX fails four times as often, mostly without touching the block.

The DAgger loop

Each round rolls the current policy out, snapshots each failure, keeps the most uncertain within a balanced budget, and lets the scripted expert record a recovery from that exact state. Recoveries are weighted ×8 in training from round 2 on.

flowchart LR
  P["current policy"] -->|800 rollouts| F["failure snapshots"]
  F -->|"uncertainty band 5–80%, budget per type"| K["kept states"]
  K -->|scripted expert| R["recoveries"]
  R -->|"×8 + base demos"| T["fine-tune 4k steps"]
  T --> B["6 suites × 96 vs v5"]
  B -->|"pooled gain, no suite worse"| P

Each round the policy fails less and the script rescues only about 40% of kept states, so recoveries shrank 85 → 72 → 59. Rounds 4 and 5 swapped in the residual RL expert, which recovered 88 of 120 and 72 of 113, but the policy got worse.

FLUX predicts well and acts badly

Open-loop the LoRA beats holding the pose three to one at 32 steps; closed-loop it succeeds 50 times in 576.

Mean action error on 60 held-out demo frames, each fed its 7 preceding frames. The quantile bridge sits inside this loop both ways, so the mapping is not the problem.

An RL expert to recover what the script cannot

A residual policy on top of the scripted expert recovers 74.0% of held-out failure snapshots; the expert alone recovers 45.9%.

Recovery from DAgger failure states the policy never trained on (W&B run fb6yv89f), with fresh scenes still at 100%. Shaped PPO from scratch stays near 6% training success and is not shown. The next DAgger round records recoveries with this expert.

What is not established

  • Nothing has run on the physical arm. The repo will be updated with live results when it arrives.
  • Round 3's new-object gain (p=0.043) is one suite of six, uncorrected for multiple comparisons, from one seed.
  • No single suite gain from round 3's retrain is significant on its own; the pooled gain passed a gate fixed in advance, twice in a row for rounds 2 and 3.
  • FLUX's failure mechanism (8-frame self-history and integrated deltas) is a hypothesis, not tested by retraining.
  • The RL expert is measured on recovering failure snapshots, not yet as a source of DAgger data.
  • One benchmark seed and one training seed per checkpoint.
