Preflight

Know whether the robot will succeed before it moves. Preflight rebuilds the table in a physics twin from one photo, rehearses the robot's own policy (NVIDIA GR00T N1.7) there dozens of times, and answers GO RETRY or ASK_HUMAN, with the evidence and an explanation from Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory.

Try the live check on your photo

Upload a top-down photo of the printed A3 mat (four corner markers, a blue block and a red block). Live, the demo runs perception and the trust gate: the markers place the twin, a Token Factory vision model reads the scene, and the gate accepts the twin or says why it can't be trusted. Rehearsal needs a GPU, so it runs for the scenes below. Uploads are limited per hour.

Rehearsed scenes

Phone frames of the mat, each run through the full pipeline on a GPU with the same container a deployment would use: synthetic frames laid out to show each verdict, and one real phone photo ("real photo") of real objects the policy was never trained on, which the trust gate refuses to rehearse. Rehearsals per scene and the 95% interval are shown with every verdict.

Across environments, objects and tasks

The served policy's benchmark: each suite is 96 rehearsals in the randomised twin (12 scenes x 8 variants), scored before the policy was promoted. Twin rehearsals, not photos. The weak rows are the honest ones: new objects are harder, and new tasks the policy was never trained on fail almost every time.
